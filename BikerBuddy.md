# Privacy Policy for BikerBuddy

**Effective date: October 22, 2026**

BikerBuddy is provided by **ACC Apps** (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains what information BikerBuddy uses, where it is stored, and when it may be shared with other services.

## Information BikerBuddy uses

BikerBuddy does not require an account. When you import a GPX or TCX ride file, the app processes the information in that file, which can include:

- GPS coordinates and route points;
- ride date and time, elevation, distance, and speed; and
- ride or segment names and related performance information.

The app derives statistics and, where available, a city and region label from route coordinates. BikerBuddy does not record your live location in the background. It processes location data contained in rides you choose to import.

## How information is stored and used

Imported ride and segment information is stored in BikerBuddy’s private app storage on your device. It is used to show your rides, calculate statistics, match segment efforts, and provide the app’s features. BikerBuddy does not operate an account system or its own service for uploading or synchronizing your ride library.

The original GPX/TCX file is read from the location you select; BikerBuddy imports its contents into the app and does not upload the source file to a BikerBuddy server. Temporary import logs may be written to the app’s cache to report import results. You can choose to share an import log with another app.

BikerBuddy does not add separate encryption to its local database. Android’s app sandbox and the security settings on your device govern access to locally stored app data.

## Maps and location lookup

Map screens load map tiles over the internet. The map tile provider may receive standard connection information, such as your device’s IP address, requested map tile coordinates (which can reveal the area being viewed), and request metadata. BikerBuddy uses the osmdroid map library and its configured tile source. OpenStreetMap tile-service information is available in the [OpenStreetMap Tile Usage Policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/) and [OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).

When BikerBuddy looks up a city or region for an imported ride, it uses Android’s Geocoder service. Depending on the device and Android implementation, that service may use a network provider. The coordinates needed for the lookup may therefore be processed by the geocoding provider selected by Android or the device manufacturer. That provider’s handling is governed by its own privacy terms; BikerBuddy does not operate the geocoding service.

Map tiles and geocoding may not be available without an internet connection. Ride data already stored on the device can otherwise be viewed and processed locally.

## Samsung Health folder access and file imports

If you choose to link a folder using Android’s folder picker, BikerBuddy stores the access grant so it can look for GPX/TCX files in that folder when you open the app. You can choose not to link a folder. To stop future scans, revoke the grant using Android’s available system controls, or clear BikerBuddy’s app data or uninstall the app. Clearing app data also deletes locally stored rides and settings. Linking a folder does not give BikerBuddy access to your Samsung Health account, and BikerBuddy does not use a Samsung Health account login or API.

You may also select individual files or open/share a file with BikerBuddy. Android grants access to the file or folder you select. If you choose to share a log or other information with another app, that app’s own privacy policy applies.

## Sharing and third parties

BikerBuddy does not sell your personal information. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. The third-party processing described in this policy is limited to services involved in loading map tiles, Android’s geocoding service, Android/device backup features, and other apps or services you explicitly choose when sharing information.

BikerBuddy’s Android manifest currently allows Android system backup and device transfer. Whether data is backed up depends on your device, Android/OEM behavior, and your backup settings. Backups are handled by the relevant Android/device service under its own terms. Consider this when storing sensitive route information.

## Retention and deletion

Your imported rides, route points, and segments remain in the app’s local storage until you delete them in the app, clear BikerBuddy’s app data, or uninstall the app. Android or device backups may retain copies according to your backup settings and the backup provider’s retention rules. Revoking access to a linked folder stops future folder scans but does not delete rides that have already been imported.

## Children’s privacy

BikerBuddy is not directed to children under 13. Parents and guardians should supervise children’s import and sharing of ride files, which may contain precise location information. If you have a privacy concern involving a child’s use of the app, contact us.

## Security

We use Android’s private app storage for locally stored ride data, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your device and its backup account protected, and avoid importing or sharing route data you do not want others to access.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or its data practices change. The updated version will be posted at the same location with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.
